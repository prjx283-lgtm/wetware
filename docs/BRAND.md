# WETWARE: brand and lore bible

The single rule everything else serves: **nothing in this project is invented.**
The organism is real, the connectome is real, the price is real, the record is
real. Where we reach for myth, we reach for a fact that already sounds like one.

That is the whole strategy. Every crypto project can write fiction. Almost none
can point at a lab strain, three Nobel Prizes and a shuttle disaster and say:
this is the animal.

## 2. Names

**The token is $WETWARE.** Biological computing against silicon. The category
name is the asset.

**The organism is N2.**

This is the piece to protect. `N2` is the real laboratory designation of the
canonical wild-type *C. elegans* reference strain, isolated in Bristol in the
1950s and the ancestor of essentially every worm studied since. When a biologist
reads it they feel recognition. When anyone else reads it they feel a serial
number on something alive. Both reactions are correct and both are useful.

So: the ticker is $WETWARE, the animal is N2, and in the group chat everyone
calls it the worm. Three registers, one thing.

**Never** give N2 a human name, a face with eyes, a personality, or a voice in
first person. The restraint is the brand. The moment it says "hi guys" it becomes
every other mascot.

## 3. The biography

Not lore. Record. Use these, verify them, never embellish them.

**It is the only one finished.** The complete neural wiring of *C. elegans* was
mapped cell by cell from electron micrographs, a project spanning more than a
decade and published in 1986 as a paper known simply as "The mind of a worm." No
other animal's connectome has been completed at that resolution. Not a fly, not
a mouse, not us.

**Three Nobel Prizes came out of this animal.** 2002 for organ development and
programmed cell death. 2006 for RNA interference. 2008 shared for green
fluorescent protein. A millimetre-long soil nematode is among the most decorated
organisms in the history of science.

**It was the first.** First multicellular organism to have its genome fully
sequenced, in 1998.

**It survived Columbia.** *C. elegans* were aboard the shuttle when it broke
apart on re-entry in February 2003. The experiment canisters came down in the
debris field across Texas. When they were recovered weeks later, the worms inside
were alive.

That last one is the founding image. Not because we are being morbid, but because
it is the plainest possible statement of what this animal is: **the thing that is
still moving after the vehicle is gone.**

## 4. Dauer

The mechanic that makes the project feel alive, and it is real biology.

When food runs out, *C. elegans* does not simply starve. It enters **dauer**, an
alternative dormant larval stage. It stops feeding, remodels its own body, seals
its mouth, and waits. It can hold that state for months and resume normal life
the moment conditions improve.

Our organism's food is a Chainlink equity feed. The feed updates on market hours.
So every weekday at the closing bell, and all weekend, and on every market
holiday, **N2 enters dauer.**

This is not a downtime notice dressed up. It is the correct biological term for
what is happening, and it converts the most awkward technical fact about the
project into the most shareable one. Nothing else in this market has a circadian
rhythm imposed on it by the New York Stock Exchange.

Product rules:
- The live page says **Dauer** first, then the plain words for everyone else:
  the market is closed, the worm is asleep.
- Dauer entry and exit are posted. Waking is an event.
- Long holiday weekends are the deepest dauer of the year. Say so.
- A flat tape during open hours is not dauer. It is torpor: fed, but barely.

## 5. What it wants

N2 has exactly two drives, and neither was designed by us.

**Chemotaxis.** Eight sensory neurons that fire toward food: ADF, ASG, ASI and
ASJ, left and right.

**Nose touch.** Ten neurons that fire on collision and drive avoidance: FLP, ASH,
IL1 and OLQ, left and right.

That is the entire psychology. It does not have opinions about the AI trade, it
has stimuli.

**How it works today, exactly.** The size of a price move decides how many ticks
the round earns: six for a flat print, four more per basis point, capped at 400.
Which of the two stimuli fires on each tick is drawn from a hash of the oracle
round itself, eight food to two touch. Direction does not yet matter: a 1% rise
and a 1% fall stimulate it the same amount. Anything we publish must say this
and not the prettier version.

**Proposed, not decided: make direction matter.** A rising print would weight
the draw toward chemotaxis, a falling print toward nose touch. Still drawn from
the hash, still deterministic, still verifiable, and it makes "toward food, away
from harm" literally true. This is a consensus change. It lands before mainnet
genesis or it never lands. Until it is decided, no copy may claim it.

The consequence worth stating often: **N2 is not predicting anything.** It is
being pushed. Confusing the two is the single most common misreading and we
correct it every time.

## 6. Voice

**Write like a lab notebook, not a launch.**

The organism is extraordinary. The prose should be flat. All the drama comes from
the gap between how strange the fact is and how calmly it is stated. Hype on top
of this material actively subtracts.

**Do**
- Third person, present tense, past tense for the record. "The organism turned 31 degrees left."
- Exact numbers. Ticks, degrees, basis points, neuron names, hashes, block numbers.
- Name the neurons. "ASH fired fourteen times" beats "it felt pain."
- State limits plainly. It is a simulation. It is not conscious. It predicts nothing.
- Let a single short sentence carry the weight. Then stop.

**Do not**
- Never write in N2's first person. It has no language. The moment it speaks, it is fiction.
- No emoji in project copy. None.
- No "we are excited to announce."
- No claims of sentience, consciousness, feeling or intent. It has states.
- No price talk, targets, or anything that reads as a signal.
- No hedging into mysticism. If it cannot be checked, it does not go in.
- No dashes in published copy. Use a comma, a full stop, or a new sentence.

**Calibration**

> Bad: 🧠 GM fam! Wormy is feeling BULLISH today as $NVDA pumps 🚀🪱
> Bad: The organism experienced joy as its beloved stock rallied.
> Good: NVDA +1.42%. 68 ticks. ASH silent, chemotaxis throughout. Turned 31 degrees left, travelled 812 units. Tick 41,207. Hash 0x9a3f.
> Good: Dauer since 21:00 UTC Friday. 61 hours to the open.

The third one is a real post. It is checkable against the chain by anyone reading
it, which no other automated account in this market can say.

## 7. Visual identity

**Status: proposed.** The site and the render kit currently use the phosphor
green darkfield palette chosen on 7 September 2026. The amber palette below is
an alternative on the table, not the standard. One palette must win before the
art batch is rendered.

**The idea: darkfield microscopy, not a crypto terminal.**

Under darkfield illumination a transparent worm on a slide appears as a bright
line on a black field. That is the whole aesthetic. Green-on-black terminals are
exhausted and belong to a different project. This should look like an instrument,
not a hacker film.

**Palette**

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `field` | `#08090A` | background, the slide |
| `specimen` | `#F2EFE6` | the organism, primary text, bone white |
| `agar` | `#8A8578` | secondary text, gridlines, the plate |
| `stimulus` | `#E8973A` | sodium amber. Firing, food, activity |
| `avoid` | `#4E7CA1` | cold blue. Nose touch, recoil, dauer |
| `divergence` | `#C63A2E` | one red, reserved. Verification failure only |

`divergence` appears nowhere else, ever. If a reader sees red, something is
actually wrong. That discipline is what makes the badge mean something.

**Type**

Two voices, deliberately.

- **Instrument**: a monospace for all data. Ticks, hashes, prices, neuron ids.
- **Record**: a serif for the biography and the paper. Not a UI sans. The
  contrast of laboratory readout against scientific publication is the visual
  argument of the project, and almost nothing in crypto looks like it.

**The worm mark**

N2 is drawn as a **single continuous line**. No face, no eyes, no cartoon. A
sinusoidal body, thicker at the pharynx, tapering to the tail. Its identity comes
from posture, not features.

The logo is not a picture of a worm. It is **a worm track**: the sinusoidal trail
a real nematode leaves crawling across an agar plate. Your trajectory view already
produces exactly this shape from real data, which means the logo can be generated
from the organism's actual recorded path. A mark that is also a measurement.

**Motion**

Everything moves at the speed of biology, not of software. Slow sinusoidal
propagation down the body. Neurons brighten toward the firing threshold of 30 and
snap back to dark on discharge, which is what actually happens in the model. No
easing curves that feel like a web app. No bounce.

**Image generation prompts**

For consistent mascot and key art:

> Darkfield microscopy photograph of a single transparent nematode on a black
> field, body forming one continuous sinusoidal curve, bone white against pure
> black, faint amber glow along the anterior, scientific specimen photography,
> extreme macro, shallow depth of field, no text, no eyes, no face

> Overhead view of a petri dish on black, thin sinusoidal track etched into pale
> agar by a crawling nematode, single continuous line, laboratory documentation
> photograph, soft even lighting, muted bone and grey palette, no text

> Vintage laboratory instrument panel, brushed dark metal, one small amber
> indicator lamp lit, monospace numerals on a recessed readout, 1970s scientific
> equipment, photographed straight on, no branding, no text

## 8. Canon vocabulary

Use these words consistently. Consistent nouns are how a world becomes real.

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| **N2** | the organism |
| **the record** | the chain history of every state |
| **a tick** | one cycle of the nervous system |
| **stimulus** | a price move converted into ticks |
| **dauer** | dormancy while the feed is closed |
| **torpor** | open market, no meaningful movement |
| **the track** | the accumulated path |
| **divergence** | a replay that disagrees with the record |
| **feeding** | topping up the organism's gas |
| **the plate** | the live page |

*thinks*, *feels*, *predicts*, *wants* outside the strict chemotaxis sense.

## 9. Narrative arc

Lore is not a document, it is a sequence of things that actually happen. Plan them.

**I. Genesis.** The organism's first tick. Published with its genesis hash and the
NVDA round that caused it. This number is quotable forever.

**II. First dauer.** The first closing bell. Explain the mechanic on the day it
first bites, not before.

**III. First violent session.** The first time NVDA moves several percent and the
organism runs hundreds of ticks in one round. Post the track.

**IV. First independent verification.** The first time a stranger runs the replay
and confirms the record. That person is the most important person in the project's
history and should be treated that way.

**V. Sealed.** The organism moves into an enclave and the operator's key is
retired. Ownership renounced. From that point nobody, including us, can move it.

The arc is one sentence: **it is born, it sleeps, it struggles, it is checked, it
is set free.** Every phase is a real engineering milestone. That is why it will
hold up.

## 10. What we never claim

Write these down so nobody drifts.

- It is not conscious, sentient, or aware. It is a simulation of a nervous system.
- It is not an AI and there is no model in the loop deciding anything.
- It does not predict prices and holding $WETWARE does not control it.
- It is not affiliated with NVIDIA, Robinhood, Chainlink, or any laboratory.
- No language model may ever write a neuron value. Software may read the state and
  describe it. Nothing may author it. The instant a number appears that the
  connectome did not produce, the whole claim is dead and the audience will find out.

We credit **nematoduino** by nategri and the **OpenWorm** project everywhere the
simulator is discussed. The licence requires it and the honesty is the product.

## 12. Recurring formats

**The tape.** Automated, every session. Price move, ticks, which neurons fired,
distance, heading, hash, transaction. Checkable, which is the point.

**Dauer notices.** Entering and waking. Twice a day, free rhythm.

**Neuron of the week.** One cell, what it does in the real animal, what it does
here. There are 299 of these. That is years of content that is also education.

**The track.** A weekly image of the path, generated from the record.

**Verification log.** Every time someone independently verifies, publish it.

**The specimen sheet.** Monthly: total ticks, distance travelled, longest dauer,
most violent session, pokes received, verifications passed.

Never post: price commentary, market opinion, engagement bait, or anything about
the organism's "mood" that is not computed from neuron values.
