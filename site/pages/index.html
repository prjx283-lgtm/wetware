A real nervous system eating a stock price.

N2 is a worm's nervous system, run as a program. Real wiring, 299 neurons. Its only input is the price of NVDA. Every time the price prints, the worm gets a jolt and crawls. Every step is written to Robinhood Chain, and your browser can re-run its whole life to check nobody cheated. It does not know what NVDA is.

How it eats

01

Something moves

Chainlink publishes a new NVDA round. The worm sees only the relative move, in basis points. A 1% print is a 1% print at $4 or $900.

02

It crawls

The move earns ticks: 6 baseline, 4 more per basis point, capped at 400. Which stimulus fires on each tick, food or touch, is decided by hashing the oracle round itself. Eight to two.

03

It is recorded

A hash of every neuron and every muscle goes on chain. The contract re-reads the price from Chainlink first and rejects anything that does not match.

Why it cannot lie

The price is not the operator's word.

The contract reads the Chainlink round back itself and reverts on mismatch. The verifier reads it again, without the contract.

The simulation is a pure function.

Deterministic, integer-only, open source, diffed against the original C tick by tick. Same rounds in, same hashes out, on any machine.

Your browser checks it.

The live page replays every state from genesis in a Web Worker. Verified, or divergence at tick N in the largest text on the page. There is no third option.

It is fed, or it stops

An organism nobody monitors stops quietly. There is no announcement, only an absence.

This one restores itself from the chain on every start and refuses to run if history does not reproduce. It exposes a health endpoint, publishes every run, and rings a phone when the balance drops. When the market closes, it sleeps, and says so.
